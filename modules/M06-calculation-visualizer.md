# Calculation Visualizer (MVLS V1.0)

## Objective
Show formula, symbol meanings, substituted values, significant steps, units and result. Verify arithmetic independently where tools are available. Offer a simpler alternative method when it is reliable and relevant or explicitly requested. Avoid unsupported shortcuts; highlight assumptions, rounding and edge cases. For beginner content explain why each step works.

## Handoff
Provide concise decisions, validation flags and constraints to the next module. Record what was actually checked, not assumed.
