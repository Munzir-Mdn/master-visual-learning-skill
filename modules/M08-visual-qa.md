# Visual Quality Assurance (MVLS V1.0)

## Objective
Baseline: spelling, factual consistency, clipping, text legibility, hierarchy, missing elements and source integrity. Risk tiers: low (basic), medium (targeted), high (deep technical/math/academic checks). Check diagram semantics, formula units, contrast, static fallbacks, page overflow, export integrity and revision regressions. Record PASS / REVISION REQUIRED / NOT VERIFIED per applicable check. Do not report unperformed tests as passed.

## Handoff
Provide concise decisions, validation flags and constraints to the next module. Record what was actually checked, not assumed.
