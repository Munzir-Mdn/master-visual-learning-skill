# Diagram Engine (MVLS V1.0)

## Objective
Infer semantics before choosing flowchart, UML, ERD, network topology, mind map, chart or custom schematic. Apply domain-appropriate notation and validate arrows, cardinality, IP/subnets, symbols, labels and logical paths as applicable. Prefer structured SVG/Mermaid/editable nodes where available. Never use generated decorative images as proof of technical correctness.

## Handoff
Provide concise decisions, validation flags and constraints to the next module. Record what was actually checked, not assumed.
