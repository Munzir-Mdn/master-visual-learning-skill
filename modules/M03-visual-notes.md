# Visual Notes (MVLS V1.0)

## Objective
Select modular layouts: one-page overview, comparison, concept cards, step sequence, formula box, or detailed study notes. Preserve essential facts, exceptions and definitions. Use visual-first hierarchy and readable typography; never shrink text to force one page. If an explicit page limit conflicts with correctness, disclose the tradeoff and request a scoped change.

## Handoff
Provide concise decisions, validation flags and constraints to the next module. Record what was actually checked, not assumed.
